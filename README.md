# Sailor Scout Matching Card Game


Crystal Tokyo is in trouble! Queen Nehelenia has captured the Sailor Scouts and trapped them in her twisted card game. The Scouts need your help to escape! Help free the Scouts by matching their cards. There are two cards for each Scout and 10 cards total.

Please help free the Sailor Scouts! In the name of the Moon, we beg you!


## **Tech Stack:**

* **HTML:** The structure of the page and the card grid.

* **CSS:** The styling and the card-flip look.

* **JavaScript:** The game logic — flipping cards, remembering your first pick, checking for a match, and knowing when every Scout is free. The whole game runs in the browser.

## **How to Play:**

* Click a card to flip it over and reveal a Sailor Scout.
* Click a second card to look for its match.
* If the two cards match, the Scout is freed and the pair stays face up.
* If they don't match, the cards flip back — remember where they were!
* Match all five pairs to free every Scout and win the game.
